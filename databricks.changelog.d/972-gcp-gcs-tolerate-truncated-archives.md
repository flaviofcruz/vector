Add the `pubsub.tolerate_truncated_archives` option (default off) to `gcp_gcs`. When enabled, the source accepts a trailing gzip/zstd `UnexpectedEof` only after verifying the full object size was read, ingests delimiter-terminated records, drops the unframed decoded tail, and permits Pub/Sub acknowledgement while emitting `ObjectStorageBlobTruncated` with the GCS bucket/key and dropped-tail byte count. Short transfers remain retryable and are not acknowledged; objects served without `Content-Length` are read without tolerance, and decode or pipeline failures keep their usual classification. Configuration rejects non-boundary framers and multiline aggregation.
