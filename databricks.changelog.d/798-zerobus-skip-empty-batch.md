Acknowledge a `databricks_zerobus` batch as delivered without ingesting when the encoder returns zero rows (the `wire_to_arrow` codec dropped every row; still counted in `wire_to_arrow_rows_dropped`). The batch reports zero sent events and emits `component_discarded_events_total` (`intentional=false`) for its rows. The SDK rejects empty batches as a non-retryable `InvalidArgument`, so such batches were marked Rejected, and sources with `delete_failed_message: false` redelivered the whole object, re-ingesting its valid rows until the message reached the DLQ.
